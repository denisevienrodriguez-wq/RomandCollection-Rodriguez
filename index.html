The Product Formulas

Three iconic finishes tailored to every aesthetic mood

Juicy Lasting Tint

A candy-coated gloss finish that intensifies over time. Formulated with rich syrup properties to give lips a plump, vivid payoff that stains evenly without drying out.

Glasting Melting Balm

An ultra-dewy, melting texture that blankets the lips in rich moisture. Offers transparent water-bomb glossiness packed with plant-derived nourishing oils.

Blur Fudge Tint

A completely matte, buttery fudge formulation that glides over lip lines seamlessly. Creates a soft-focus, airbrushed gradient look with zero weight or flaking.
